PRIVACY
POLICY

Last updated: August 2026. BETHWOLF Industries. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use TamilKalyanam we collect the following information:

  • Phone number — used for account verification and login
  • Profile information — name, age, gender, caste, education, occupation, location, physical details, family details, habits, dosham, horoscope preference
  • Photos — profile photo uploaded by the user
  • Chat messages — stored securely for 24 hour access period
  • Payment transaction IDs — for account verification only, not card details
  • Activity data — likes, profile views, connect history

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Your phone number is used for OTP verification and account security only
  • Profile information is displayed to other users for matrimony matching purposes
  • Photos are displayed on your profile and in search results
  • Chat messages are stored temporarily for the 24 hour chat access period
  • Activity data is used to show you likes received and profiles who viewed you
  • We never sell your personal data to third parties
  • We never use your data for advertising purposes

3. Data Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers located in Singapore. Supabase uses industry standard encryption and security protocols. Payment processing is handled entirely by Razorpay — we never store your card details or banking information on our servers.

4. Phone Number Privacy

Your phone number is never displayed publicly on the platform. Phone numbers are shared with other users only through the mutual consent request system inside the chat — both parties must agree before any number is shared. Direct sharing of phone numbers in chat messages is automatically detected and blocked by our safety system.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with any third parties except:

  • When required by Indian law or valid court order
  • When requested by cyber crime or law enforcement authorities
  • When required to investigate reports of illegal activity on the platform

In all other cases your data remains private and is never shared, sold, or transferred.

6. App Permissions

TamilKalyanam requests only the minimum permissions required to function:

  • Internet access — required to connect to our servers
  • Camera and storage — optional, only for uploading profile photos
  • Notifications — optional, for receiving alerts about likes and messages

We do not access your contacts, location, microphone, or any other device data.

7. Your Rights

Under PDPB 2023 guidelines you have the following rights:

  • Right to access your personal data at any time
  • Right to correct inaccurate information via Edit Profile
  • Right to delete your account and all associated data permanently
  • Right to withdraw consent by deleting your account

To exercise any of these rights contact us at support@tamilkalyanam.com. Account deletion can also be done directly from the app in Profile — Settings — Delete Account.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion all profile data, chat messages, likes, and activity history is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days. Payment records may be retained as required by Indian tax and financial laws.

9. Children's Privacy

TamilKalyanam is strictly for users 18 years and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware that a user is below the legal age we will immediately delete their account and all associated data.

10. Contact For Privacy

For any privacy concerns, data requests, or complaints contact BETHWOLF Industries at support@tamilkalyanam.com. We respond within 72 hours on working days.
